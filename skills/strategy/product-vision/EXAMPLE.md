# Example: product-vision

## Input

> Write a product vision for our tool that helps small online sellers manage
> shipping and returns.

## Output

# ShipEasy — Product Vision

## Vision statement
A one-person shop ships like a global retailer. The moment an order comes in, the cheapest right label is already chosen, the customer already knows where their parcel is, and a return is one tap that resolves itself — so the seller spends their day making and selling, not standing in line at the post office or answering "where's my order?".

## The customer & the change
For **solo and very small online sellers** who today lose hours to shipping logistics and dread returns. When we've won, fulfillment stops being a tax on their time and a source of anxiety — it runs quietly in the background, and a great post-purchase experience becomes something even the smallest shop can offer.

## Why it matters
The hardest part of running a small product business shouldn't be the logistics that big competitors have automated away. Leveling that playing field lets more people make a living from what they make.

## What this rules in / out
- **In:** automating carrier choice, proactive tracking comms, self-serve returns. **Out (for now):** warehouse/3PL tooling for large operations, B2B freight — that's a different customer whose world we're not describing here.
- A feature that helps big enterprises but not the solo seller doesn't serve this vision — easy no.
