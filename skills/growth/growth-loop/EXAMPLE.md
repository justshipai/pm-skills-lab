# Example: growth-loop

## Input

> We're a tool that turns voice memos into polished blog posts. Leadership wants a
> growth plan and is asking which channels to invest in. Design how we grow.

## Output

# VoiceToPost — Growth Loop

*(The ask was "which channels" — but channels are a funnel. Here's the loop that could make growth compound; channels feed it.)*

## The loop
1. User records a voice memo → 2. We generate a polished blog post → 3. User publishes it (with a subtle "Made with VoiceToPost" + the post is public) → 4. The post ranks in search / gets shared and read → 5. Some readers are would-be writers who see the attribution / quality → 6. They sign up → back to step 1.

**Content/UGC loop:** the product's output (published posts) *is* the acquisition channel.

## Loop type & engine
**Content loop**, powered by user-generated published posts that are themselves discoverable. Secondary thread: mild virality via the attribution link (not the core engine).

## Does it compound?
It compounds if **published posts reliably drive ≥1 new activated writer over their lifetime** (a content "k" ≥ 1 through SEO/shares). That depends on: posts being public + indexable, quality high enough to rank, and attribution converting readers. Realistically the per-post yield is < 1, so it's a **compounding-but-sub-viral loop** — it lowers blended CAC and grows with the content base rather than exploding. Be honest: it's an engine that strengthens over time, not overnight virality.

## The constraint
The rate-limiter is **step 3 → publish-public**: if users keep posts private or strip attribution, the loop breaks. Maximizing the share of generated posts that get published publicly (with attribution) is the single highest-leverage lever — more than top-of-funnel ads.

## Cycle time
Slow: SEO/discovery takes weeks-to-months per post to compound. So pair it with a faster acquisition channel for near-term volume while the content base (and the loop) builds.

## Optimize first / secondary loop
Optimize **publish-public rate** first (the constraint). Secondary loop: an invite/collaboration loop (writers invite editors/co-authors) — explore only after the content loop is working. Channels (ads, communities) are fuel to prime the loop, not the engine itself.
