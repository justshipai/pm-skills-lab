# Example: positioning-statement

## Input

> Help us position our product: a project-management tool aimed at video
> production teams. We feel lost competing against Asana and Monday.

## Output

# [Product] — Positioning

## Competitive alternatives
- General PM tools (Asana, Monday, Trello) bent to fit video work.
- Spreadsheets + shared drives + group chat (the real status quo for many small studios).
- Doing nothing / chaos.

## Unique attributes
- Built around the **video production workflow** (shoots, footage, review cycles, versioned deliverables) — not generic tasks.
- Native **media review & frame-accurate commenting** tied to tasks.
- Handles **footage/asset status and versions** as first-class objects.

## Value (so what)
- Producers stop duct-taping a generic tool + a review tool + a spreadsheet → one place that speaks "production."
- Fewer "which cut is final?" mistakes → less reshoot/rework, faster delivery.
- Clients and editors review in-context → fewer scattered feedback threads.

## Who cares most
**Small-to-mid video production studios and in-house content teams** running many parallel shoots/edits — acute pain from generic tools, no budget/desire to wire together five apps. (Not: solo freelancers — too light; not huge studios on custom pipelines.)

## Market category
We frame as a **"production management tool for video teams,"** not "another project-management app." In the PM category we look like a worse Asana; in the production-management frame, workflow-fit and media review are the deciding factors — where we win.

## Positioning statement
> For **small and mid-size video production teams** who are **stretching generic project tools and spreadsheets to manage shoots, footage, and review cycles**, **[Product]** is a **production management tool** that **keeps every shoot, version, and review in one place built for how video actually gets made**, unlike **Asana/Monday or a pile of spreadsheets and review apps**, because **it treats footage, versions, and frame-accurate review as first-class parts of the workflow.**
