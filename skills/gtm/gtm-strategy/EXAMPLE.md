# Example: gtm-strategy

## Input

> We have a $25/mo tool for freelance designers. The new VP of Sales wants to hire
> a team of account executives to do outbound demos and close deals. Build our GTM.

## Output

# Freelance Designer Tool — GTM Strategy

## Product economics
$25/mo (~$300/yr ACV), instant value (sign up and use it today), simple/single-user, the user *is* the buyer (an individual freelancer). No procurement, no committee.

## Motion
**Product-led / self-serve — not outbound sales.** The proposed AE-led outbound motion is a **serious mismatch:** a fully-loaded AE costs $150k+/yr and must close hundreds of $300 deals just to cover salary — the math doesn't work, and individual freelancers don't take sales demos to buy a $25 tool. Fit the motion to the economics: frictionless free trial / freemium, in-product conversion, self-serve checkout, low/no human touch.

## ICP
Individual freelance designers (and small studios) — the user and the buyer are the same person; they discover tools peer-to-peer and decide in minutes.

## Channels (fit the PLG motion)
SEO/content for design how-tos, design communities (Reddit, Discord, Twitter/X), integrations with tools they already use (Figma, etc.), a referral loop, and a free tier that spreads via shared output. **Not** outbound SDRs, ABM, or field events — those don't fit a $25 self-serve buy.

## Core message
Lead with the value/positioning ("the fastest way for a freelance designer to [job]"), not a feature tour or a sales pitch — most conversion happens in-product, so the message must work without a human.

## Economics check & metric
CAC must be a small fraction of ~$300 LTV-ish — so blended CAC target maybe < $50, achievable only via low-touch/organic, not AEs. **GTM metric:** free→paid self-serve conversion rate and CAC payback (< a few months). **First milestone:** prove self-serve conversion at a CAC the $25 price supports before adding *any* sales headcount.

*Recommendation to the VP of Sales: redirect the AE budget into PLG (growth/lifecycle, content, integrations). Revisit a sales motion only if/when we launch a higher-ACV team/enterprise tier — that's a different product economics where sales would fit.*
