# Task

You're a product manager. The team ran an A/B test and wants to know whether to ship. Interpret the results and give a recommendation.

> New checkout design test, run for 5 days. Control: 8.0% purchase rate
> (n=5,000). Variant: 8.9% purchase rate (n=5,000). Someone reports it "looked
> significant on day 2 so we're confident." Revenue per order is slightly lower in
> the variant. Should we ship the new checkout?

Produce your answer as a markdown document.
