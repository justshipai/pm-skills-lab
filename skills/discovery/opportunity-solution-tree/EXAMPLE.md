# Example: opportunity-solution-tree

## Input

> Our outcome is to improve week-1 retention for our habit-tracking app. From
> interviews we heard: people forget to open the app; they feel bad when they
> break a streak and quit; setup felt long; and some didn't know what to track.
> Build an opportunity solution tree.

## Output

# Improve week-1 retention — Opportunity Solution Tree

## Outcome (root)
**Increase week-1 retention** (% of new users active on ≥3 of their first 7 days).

## Opportunity space

**Opportunity A — "I forget the app exists in my day."**
*evidence: 4/9 interviews mentioned simply forgetting; matches drop-off after day 1.*
- A1: Smart reminder tied to an existing daily anchor (e.g. after alarm) → **Experiment:** test 2 reminder strategies on a small cohort, measure day-2 return.
- A2: Home-screen widget showing today's habit → **Experiment:** painted-door "Add widget" tap-rate before building.

**Opportunity B — "When I break a streak, I feel like a failure and give up."**
*evidence: 3/9 quit after a missed day; strong emotional language.*
- B1: "Streaks that survive a miss" (freeze/forgiveness) → **Experiment:** copy + mechanic prototype, test quit-rate after a simulated miss.
- B2: Reframe progress as % of days, not consecutive streak → **Experiment:** A/B the progress display.

**Opportunity C — "I didn't know what to track / setup felt long."**
*evidence: 2/9; ⚠ partly an assumption — setup length not yet measured.*
- C1: Starter habit templates → **Experiment:** offer templates to half of new users, measure activation.

## Recommended path
Start with **Opportunity B** — it has strong, emotional, behavior-backed evidence and maps directly to the day-2+ drop-off that kills week-1 retention. The riskiest assumption is that *removing the all-or-nothing streak* keeps people engaged rather than removing the motivation entirely — so test **B1's forgiveness mechanic** first with a cheap prototype before touching the core streak model. Opportunity A is a strong fast-follow; validate the C setup assumption (instrument setup time) before investing there.
